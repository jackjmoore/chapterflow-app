Continuity · how relationships read · fake data

1 keeps everything on the line but makes it say which way it reads. The label sits along its own edge, always upright, and an arrowhead points from the entry the label starts at towards the other. A line with no arrowhead reads the same in both directions.

2 stops asking the map to carry the words. The lines show the shape of the cast, and the relationships are written out as complete sentences beside it. Pointing at either one lights the other, so the picture and the reading stay tied together.

3 shows one entry's relationships at a time. Choosing a name dims the rest of the map and writes that entry's links out underneath in full, including how each one reads back the other way, which is the part the old labels left out entirely.
