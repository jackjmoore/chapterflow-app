Continuity board · fake data

A is the thread. The chronology stays one ordered spine, but the in-story date gets a column of its own on the left, so the board can be read down its dates as well as its events. Broken links move out of the cards into one notice at the top, where the tidy-up already lives.

B is the lanes. The same events, turned ninety degrees: one lane per person, place or object, and a bead wherever that item appears. It answers the question a list cannot, which is who is present when, and where somebody drops out of the story. Choosing a bead opens its event underneath.

C is two panes. The order stays in a narrow list on the left and the chosen event opens beside it, editable where it sits rather than in a dialog. Relationships get the same treatment, so both faces of the board work the same way.
