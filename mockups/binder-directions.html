A — the rail. Today's tree, tightened: Draft / Notes / Matter become quiet section labels instead of folder rows, giving every real row one level of indent back. Status stays a dot, tags stay chips, split/delete only appear on hover (try it). No word counts — the binder stays a place for names, as it is now.

B — the ledger. Two-line rows: chapter number in its own column, the synopsis under the title, the live word count always visible on the right, and status as a colored left edge instead of a dot. Folders carry rolled-up counts. Densest in information, tallest in rows — the binder becomes half an outliner.

C — the contents page. Typography does all the work: manuscript documents in the page's own serif with dot leaders out to their word counts, parts as small caps with rolled-up folios, sections as centered rules. Status keeps its dot but shrinks; Notes and Matter drop to quiet sans lists — only the manuscript is a page.
